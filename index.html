      How the AI detection engine works

      A hybrid architecture combining high-speed statistical Natural Language Processing with deep structural threat heuristics.

      01

      NLP Preprocessing

      Text normalization, entity masking (currency, URLs, emails), and tokenization into salient unigrams and bigrams.

      02

      Machine Learning

      A Multinomial Naive Bayes & TF-IDF model calculates Bayesian posterior log-odds across 250+ calibrated linguistic features.

      03

      Explainable AI (XAI)

      Feature importance attribution maps top positive (spam) and negative (legitimate) tokens with their mathematical impact weights.

      04

      Structural Heuristics

      24 inspection rules evaluate brand lookalikes, reply-to rerouting, affiliate parameters, and fake conversation threads.

      05

      Ensemble Meta-Verdict

      Statistical ML probability is synthesized with structural risk to yield a definitive, highly calibrated classification.

      24 threat & spam categories

      Structured into critical security threats and subtle unsolicited commercial spam patterns.

      Critical Security & Fraud Vectors

      Brand Impersonation

      Email references major brands (PayPal, Amazon, Apple, Microsoft) while originating from an unaligned third-party domain.

      Credential Phishing

      Explicit requests for passwords, OTPs, PINs, or account verification — never legitimate over email.

      Business Email Compromise

      Executive impersonation demanding urgent confidential wire transfers or gift card purchases under secrecy.

      Malware Delivery

      Dangerous attachments (.exe, .ps1, .docm), macro activation instructions, or password-protected archives.

      Lookalike Domains

      Character substitutions (paypa1, arnazon), deceptive hyphens, or typosquatted brand domains.

      Suspicious Sender Domain

      Free webmail services (Gmail, Yahoo) claiming official corporate authority, or registered on high-risk TLDs.

      Security Urgency & Threats

      High-pressure account suspension or compliance deadlines compelling hasty unverified actions.

      Personal Data Harvesting

      Requests for SSN, date of birth, passport, or full credit card coordinates under identity verification pretexts.

      Financial Bait / Prize Scams

      Lottery sweepstakes notifications, crypto doubling, or unclaimed inheritance lures.

      Header Anomalies

      Reply-To header routing responses to a different server than the sender, or display name masquerading.

      Advance-Fee & Romance Fraud

      Classic 419 schemes, dying widow stories, overseas fund transfers, or spiritual blessing hooks.

      Fake Invoice & Shipping

      Fabricated courier delivery fees (DHL, FedEx, USPS) or fictitious invoice balances to trigger link clicks.

      Subtle Spam, Bulk Marketing & Outreach Manipulation

      Unsolicited B2B Cold Outreach

      Unsolicited sales pitches, lead generation offers, SEO guest post requests, or "quick 15-minute call" solicitations.

      Promotional & Discount Lures

      Mass marketing claims ("80% discount", "special promo code", "exclusive voucher") with embedded tracking links.

      Deceptive Subject Lines

      Pretending an ongoing relationship with fake "Re:" or "Fwd:" prefixes on cold emails, and manipulative clickbait.

      Artificial Scarcity & FOMO

      Fabricated scarcity triggers ("only 3 items left", "expires midnight", "doors closing") to rush recipient actions.

      Work-from-Home / Job Scams

      Unrealistic data entry or part-time roles promising $300-$500/day with Telegram/WhatsApp recruitment contacts.

      Webinar & Survey Bait

      Unsolicited masterclass registrations or short consumer surveys baiting users with free gift card rewards.

      Greymail & Opt-Out Evasion

      Pseudo opt-out/unsubscribe notices sent via personal webmail accounts to evade corporate spam filters.

      Tracking & Affiliate URLs

      Detection of affiliate attribution parameters (?ref=, ?aff=, utm_campaign) and bulk-email redirector hops.

      Structural Bulk Signals

      Disproportionately high link density compared to message length, or blind distribution to "undisclosed-recipients".

      Commercial Hype & Hyperbole

      Exaggerated claims ("100% guaranteed results", "revolutionary secret system", "featured on Forbes").

      Generic Salutations

      Impersonal greetings ("Dear Valued Shopper", "Dear Friend") characteristic of mass bulk mailing.

      Formatting Abuse

      Aggressive block capitals, repeating exclamation marks, or stacked attention-seeking symbols.
